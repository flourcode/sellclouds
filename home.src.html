How these work

Every tool here is five questions or a few numbers. Each takes about a minute, runs in your browser, and stores nothing.

None of them need an account. None of them are AI. They're the questions a good VP asks in a review, asked before the VP does, with a straight answer at the end and one thing to go do. They grew out of Deal Check, which is still the one to start with.

More are coming. They're free. Use whatever helps.

Questions

Who is this for?
People selling complicated technology (cloud, AI, cybersecurity, data, infrastructure) to government, and the managers and partner people around them. Most of the tools work the same in enterprise; the federal-specific ones say so.
Does anything I enter leave my device?
No. Every tool runs in your browser. No account, no CRM connection, no API call. The site counts page views with Google Analytics and never sends your answers or your numbers. Nothing else leaves the page unless you choose to share a result.
Is any of this AI?
No. Every tool is plain questions and plain arithmetic, the same answers every time, and each one shows its work.
What does it cost?
The tools are free. If you want a second set of eyes on something, the first conversation is free too.
What happened to Kill My Deal?
It's Deal Check now, at sellclouds.com/deal/. Same five questions, a friendlier name. Every old killmydeal.com link, including shared verdicts, still lands in the right place.

SellClouds is a shelf of free tools by Mark Flournoy.
